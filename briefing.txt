PRIVATE EQUITY OVERLAY

Financing. 3M Euribor around 2.75-2.80% and moving higher; term SOFR at 3.63%. The ECB hike is the more material variable for euro-denominated deals: all-in senior rates are rising for the first time this cycle, directly compressing deal economics. Leveraged loan refinancing volumes fell roughly 40% year-on-year in Q1 2026, limiting the escape valve sponsors used in 2024-25. Private credit funds about 80% of LBO financing; direct lenders are repricing in line with base rates. HY spreads at 285bp keep absolute all-in yields elevated even with tight OAS.

Comps and exits. Energy and materials EV/EBITDA multiples are compressing on the oil selloff. Defence, infrastructure, and industrial names remain elevated on geopolitical themes. The IPO window is cautious; PE sponsors continue to rely on continuation vehicles and secondary sales as primary exit routes.

Deal flow. No headline sponsor transactions overnight. Rising euro base rates, soft US manufacturing data, and an oil correction are likely to keep sponsors cautious on new large-cap LBOs in the near term.

The ECB hike and Warsh's hawkish pivot are a genuine pricing event this week: euro financing costs are rising for the first time this cycle, reducing available deal leverage and pressuring exit multiples in rate-sensitive sectors such as real estate, consumer, and infrastructure.
