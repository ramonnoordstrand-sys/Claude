Sunday 28 June 2026 - Morning Briefing (CET)

The mood heading into Monday is cautious and risk-off. A sharp global selloff in AI and semiconductor stocks drove the Nikkei down more than 4% on Friday, and the Federal Reserve's June dot plot delivered the most hawkish surprise since 2022, signalling a potential rate hike rather than the cuts markets had priced. The partial offset is oil: Brent has collapsed more than 10% on the week to around $72 as Strait of Hormuz transits resume and US-Iran peace talks advance. The most important data point for the week is the ongoing repricing of the US rate path following Friday's in-line PCE print, which cooled hike expectations at the margin but did not reverse the broader hawkish shift.

PRIVATE EQUITY OVERLAY

Healthcare remains the most resilient sector for deployment.

KKR also reported over $900m in monetisation income in Q2, signalling that large-cap exit recovery continues even as mid-market activity is subdued. Healthcare take-privates and platform roll-ups are maintaining pace.

Bottom line: The hawkish Fed pivot is the week's key regime change - it raises the risk-free rate embedded in LBO underwriting, increases floating-rate debt service costs for leveraged portfolio companies, and compresses the private/public valuation gap. The oil collapse is a net positive for consumer-facing and industrial holdings but reduces carry for energy positions.
