MORNING MARKETS BRIEFING - Monday 16 June 2026 (CET)
----------------------------------------------------

Risk firmly on this morning after the US and Iran confirmed a permanent peace
agreement on Sunday, with a formal signing in Switzerland set for Friday 19
June. The deal reopens the Strait of Hormuz, lifts the US naval blockade on
Iranian ports, suspends oil sanctions, and releases $24bn in frozen assets.
Asia surged and oil is sharply lower. The main macro events today are US Empire
State Manufacturing (14:30 CET) and Industrial Production (15:15 CET).

BOTTOM LINE

The Iran peace deal is the single largest regime change since the conflict
began in February: if it holds, it unwinds the energy inflation shock that
drove the ECB's June hike, relieves pressure on floating-rate debt costs,
and widens the exit window for industrial and energy-exposed portfolio companies,
but oil normalisation will take months and geopolitical execution risk remains
until the 19 June signing.
