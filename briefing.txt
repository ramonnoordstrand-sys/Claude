MORNING BRIEFING -- 25 June 2026

Risk tone is defensive heading into the European open: the dollar sits at a 13-month high, gold is at 8-month lows, and European equity futures are softer. The day's pivotal release is US May PCE at 14:30 CET, where headline inflation is expected to print 4.1% year-on-year -- a number that will sharpen or ease the sharp repricing of September Fed hike odds that has taken place this week.

PRIVATE EQUITY OVERLAY

Financing: ECB deposit rate now around 2.0-2.25% post-June hike; 3m Euribor likely 2.3-2.5%. Term SOFR (3m) approximately 4.3-4.4% with the Fed on hold. Private credit is absorbing deal flow that leveraged loan markets are not supporting. HY spreads at 285 bps push all-in costs on new HY paper to roughly 8-9%. Deals are clearing, principally in healthcare, but existing floating-rate debt is under pressure as interest coverage on the US leveraged loan index has fallen to around 4.6x versus 6x in 2022.

Comps and exits: Tech and consumer public EV/EBITDA multiples are compressed amid AI-driven valuation uncertainty and margin pressure. Healthcare and infrastructure are resilient. IPO windows remain narrow. GP-led secondaries and continuation vehicles are the primary liquidity mechanism. PE deal volume in H1 2026 is roughly 34% below H1 2025 by count, though average deal size has risen sharply as capital concentrates in high-conviction bets.

Deal flow: Healthcare take-privates and platform roll-ups are the most active segment. No significant new transactions confirmed overnight. Take-privates and carve-outs remain the main source of large-cap flow given ample dry powder and private credit capacity.

Bottom line: A hot PCE print at 14:30 CET today would cement September hike pricing, drive the dollar and yields higher, and further tighten financing conditions for portfolio companies -- watch the reaction in HY spreads and EUR/USD. Iran peace-process fragility is the key tail risk on the geopolitical side.
