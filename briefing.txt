PRIVATE EQUITY OVERLAY

Financing
3M Euribor running above 2.40% post the ECB hike; term SOFR near 3.75%. First-lien private credit all-in yields expected to trough around 8.0-8.5%. Leveraged loan spreads are near historic tights (B/BB paper in the L+300-350bp area), so deals are clearing, but the ECB hike adds 25bp to the EUR base rate floor and squeezes European LBO economics on the margin. Direct lending remains open and competitive.

Comps and exits
Tech and software public multiples are holding firm after the semiconductor rally, supporting sponsor exit valuations in digital sectors. US IPO market is improving: 46 deals raised $28.2B through end-May, more than double a year ago. US M&A value reached $1.2 trillion in the first five months, nearly double H1 2025, on fewer but larger transactions. Sponsors continue to lean on continuation vehicles and secondaries over traditional exits.

BOTTOM LINE
The ECB hike has raised EUR financing costs and is squeezing European LBO returns at the margin; Lagarde's 15:00 CET parliamentary testimony is the day's market event to watch, and any signal of further hikes would reprice both cost of debt and NAV discount rates across European portfolios.
